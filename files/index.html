About Strand & Co.

Every strand is knotted by hand.

We started with a handful of beads and a spool of cord, and grew into a small studio that makes bracelets and necklaces meant for everyday wear — not display cases. No gold, no gemstones behind glass. Just good beads, honest cord, and pieces you can layer, stack and actually live in.

Every order is packed and knotted individually, and every design started as something we wanted to wear ourselves.
